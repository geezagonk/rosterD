Rostered email templates

Rendered against the demo register. Every figure in these comes out of the contractor record, not a placeholder.

  • Extension request to agency Signals intent to extend, asks for a rate confirmation and a revised schedule. Send inside the notice window.
  • Non-extension / end of engagement Confirms the engagement will end on the contracted date and sets out the exit steps.
  • PO variation / burn warning Flags that the purchase order is close to exhausted and asks the vendor to hold invoicing until a variation is raised.
  • Pre-start readiness chase Chases the outstanding pre-start items so day one is not wasted. Lists exactly what is still open.
  • Work rights evidence request Requests renewed right-to-work evidence before the current entitlement lapses.
  • Tenure and engagement status review Internal note flagging long tenure and the co-employment / permanence question.
  • Vendor agreement renewal Opens the renewal or retender conversation ahead of the master agreement lapsing.
  • Offboarding instruction (internal) Tells the service desk, security and finance exactly what to switch off and when.
  • Rate benchmark challenge Puts a charge rate that sits above the benchmark back to the agency, with the numbers attached.
  • Welcome and day one logistics Goes to the contractor directly. Start time, who to ask for, what they need to bring.
  • Approval chase to an approver Polite, specific, and impossible to ignore: what it is, how long you have had it, what happens if it slips further.
  • Invoice approval chase (internal) Chases invoices sitting past the internal approval service level, with the consequence spelled out.
  • Payment chase to Accounts Payable For approved invoices that have gone past their due date. Includes the ageing so nobody argues about it.
  • Missing invoice chase to supplier For periods that have closed with no invoice. Frames it as an accrual problem, which tends to get a faster response.
  • Credit note request For an invoice that does not match the contracted terms. States the discrepancy and what you need back.
  • Weekly position update to the manager The regular reporting bullet, as an email. Numbers, decisions coming up, and what is stuck.
